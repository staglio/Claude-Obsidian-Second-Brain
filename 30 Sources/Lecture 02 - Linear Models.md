## Summary
- Linear regression predicts a number with a weighted sum of features.
- Logistic regression reuses the weighted sum and adds a sigmoid to output a probability.
- Training = minimizing a loss function; gradient descent does it step by step.
- The learning rate and feature scaling decide whether gradient descent converges quickly.
